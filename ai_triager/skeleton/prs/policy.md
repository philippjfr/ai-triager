## Contributing with AI tools

- Follow the repository's PR template.
- Explain what motivated the change; do not just list what changed.
- Include reproduction steps and, for visual changes, before and after screenshots.
- Disclose AI usage and take responsibility for all generated content.
- Do not paste an AI-generated summary as the description.
